隐私权政策 / Privacy Policy

本工具仅供账号持有人本人使用。它只在本人的 Google 云端硬盘中读写它自己创建的文件（drive.file 权限），不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人信息，也不向任何第三方传输数据。

This tool is used only by the account owner. It only reads and writes files it created in the owner's own Google Drive (drive.file scope). It does not collect, store, or share any personal information, and transmits no data to any third party.
